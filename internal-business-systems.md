# Internal Business Systems

> Proprietary project — case study only, no source code.

## Overview

Internal systems and tools that support day-to-day business operations.

**Problem:** Internal teams need tailored tools for administration and operations that off-the-shelf software does not cover.

**Solution:** A web-based system developed by the team to support these processes, which I contributed to as a full-stack developer.

## My Role

Full-Stack Developer in a development team. The system architecture and technical direction were defined by the team and technical leads; I implemented features across the frontend, backend, and database within that architecture.

- API development and integration
- Maintenance of existing tools
